A premium dark-mode SaaS marketing landing page for ProxxyMedia — a social media account management platform. The page should feel electric, confident, and high-end, like a product people aspire to use. Deep obsidian background. Neon-indigo as the dominant accent. Glassmorphism card surfaces. Everything communicates power, clarity, and control.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop-first (1440px max-width container)
- Theme: Dark mode — deep obsidian backgrounds, glassmorphism card surfaces
- Background: Deep Obsidian (#0A0B14) page canvas; Midnight Slate (#12141F) card/surface
- Primary Accent: Electric Indigo (#6C47FF) — buttons, highlights, active states
- Secondary Accent: Neon Violet (#9B59F5) — gradient partner to indigo
- Highlight: Cyber Cyan (#00D4FF) — data points, sparklines, inline callouts
- Text Primary: Pure Cloud White (#F0F2FF)
- Text Secondary: Muted Lavender (#8B8FA8)
- Success: Mint Green (#00E5A0)
- Border/Glass Edge: rgba(255,255,255,0.06) with 1px border
- Font: Inter — 700 bold headings, 400 body, 500 medium labels
- Buttons: Pill-shaped (border-radius: 9999px), Electric Indigo fill for primary, ghost outline for secondary
- Cards: Glassmorphism — semi-transparent dark surface, subtle indigo-tinted border, soft diffused shadow
- Layout: Generous whitespace, centered content at max-width, 12-column grid

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**

1. **Sticky Navigation Bar:**
   - Left: "ProxxyMedia" logotype in bold white, with a small indigo lightning-bolt icon prefix
   - Center: Links — Features, Pricing, Blog, Customers
   - Right: "Log in" ghost button + "Start Free Trial" pill-shaped Electric Indigo primary CTA button
   - Glassmorphism background on scroll (backdrop-blur with dark tint)

2. **Hero Section:**
   - Large centered headline (72px bold): "One Workspace. Every Account. Full Control."
   - Subheadline (20px, Muted Lavender): "ProxxyMedia connects all your social accounts in one powerful dashboard — schedule posts, track analytics, and manage your inbox across every platform."
   - Two CTAs: "Start Free — No Card Required" (primary indigo pill) + "Watch Demo →" (ghost text link with arrow)
   - Hero visual: A floating glassmorphism dashboard mockup showing connected platform icons (Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok) with live metric cards (followers, reach, engagement) — subtle glow behind the card
   - Faint indigo radial gradient glow centered behind the hero card

3. **Social Proof Bar:**
   - Single horizontal strip: "Trusted by 12,000+ agencies and creators"
   - Six greyscale partner/customer logos in a marquee strip

4. **Features Grid (3 columns):**
   - Section title: "Everything you need to dominate social media"
   - Six feature cards (glassmorphism style):
     1. **Multi-Account Hub** — Connect Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube. Cyan icon.
     2. **Visual Content Calendar** — Drag-and-drop scheduling with platform-specific previews. Indigo icon.
     3. **Unified Inbox** — All comments, DMs, and mentions in one place. Violet icon.
     4. **Deep Analytics** — Reach, engagement, follower growth — all in real-time charts. Cyan icon.
     5. **AI Caption Writer** — Generate on-brand captions and hashtags instantly. Indigo icon.
     6. **Team Collaboration** — Roles, approvals, and activity logs for your whole team. Mint icon.

5. **Dashboard Preview Section:**
   - Full-width dark section with a large browser-frame screenshot mockup of the ProxxyMedia dashboard
   - Left-aligned headline: "Your entire social presence, in one view."
   - Floating annotation callout cards pointing to: scheduling queue, analytics chart, inbox counter

6. **Pricing Teaser:**
   - 3-column cards: Starter ($29/mo), Pro ($79/mo), Agency ($199/mo)
   - Pro card has indigo glassmorphism glow and "Most Popular" badge
   - Each card: plan name, price, 4 key features, CTA button

7. **Final CTA Section:**
   - Dark indigo gradient section
   - Headline: "Ready to take control of your social media?"
   - Subtext + "Start Free Trial" primary button + "See Pricing →" text link

8. **Footer:**
   - Dark footer, ProxxyMedia logo, 4-column links (Product, Company, Resources, Legal)
   - Social icons, copyright line
